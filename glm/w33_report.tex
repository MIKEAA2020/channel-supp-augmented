\noindent\textbf{Charge and method.} The full seven-step proof was re-read
line by line and re-derived by hand a second time, with the repairs in
place; this reading was explicitly a \emph{seam} reading --- each repaired
passage was checked in context, against its neighbours, for dangling
dependencies, contradictions with unrepaired text, and claims silently
changed by the patches. Every checkable arithmetic claim was re-computed by
a fresh exact-integer script (\texttt{w33\_second\_pass\_check.py},
\textbf{178 checks, all PASS}, exit 0) written from scratch for this pass,
independently of the manuscript, of the machine data, and of the
w30/w31/w32 verifier scripts. Four bugs in the checker's own code were
caught and fixed en route (a shadowed function name, an orbit-basis
ordering assumption, a wrong kernel-basis convention in the Smith-normal-form
routine, and a degree mismatch in the $\sigma_2$-bookkeeping evaluation);
each had produced spurious failures against mathematics that re-derivation
confirmed was correct.

\section*{1. Verdict, up front}

\textbf{The repairs read cleanly end-to-end.} The v12 proof is now a
coherent, self-consistent, line-checkable derivation from Step~0 to
Step~7, and the referee cycle of Waves 30--32 is closed. All eleven repair
hunks are present (R1's five from Wave~31; R2--R7's six from Wave~32);
all seven superseded phrasings are gone; every load-bearing claim ---
including every claim the repairs introduced --- was independently
re-verified, and the cross-step dependencies that the repairs created or
relied on all resolve.

\textbf{No mandatory corrections.} Two residual polish items are recorded
below (Findings F1 and F2): each is a single optional clause, neither
affects any assertion of the Proposition, and neither rises to the level of
a repair. In journal terms: \emph{accept; the minor revision has been
executed and executed well.}

The epistemic chain on the Proposition now runs: hand-derived (W29),
machine-certified (13C), independently re-implemented (W28),
referee-verified (W30, findings R1--R7), lattice-gated (W31, R1 applied
plus the G3L gate), referee-complete (W32, R2--R7 adjudicated and applied),
and now \textbf{second-pass confirmed} (W33, this wave).

\section*{3. Findings (optional; one clause each; no mathematics affected)}

\textbf{F1 (nano, presentational).} Step~1's repaired text uses
$\sigma_1$, $\sigma_2$, $\tau$ --- and the transgression value
$\sigma_2+2\tau^2$ that Step~4 pins --- with first occurrences at source
lines 1931/1933, while $\sigma_i$ is introduced at line 1969, $\tau$ at
line 1983, and the value pinned at line 2047: thirty-eight lines of forward
dependency. The odd-$r$ clause carries its marker (``by Step~3 below'');
the even-$r$ clause carries none. There is no logical circularity (Steps
2--4 are established by independent means), but a linear reader meets the
symbols before their definitions; one parenthetical would close it. In the
same sentence, the lead-in ``the fibre classes there are \emph{boundaries}
before the $E_8$-page'' conflates two death modes --- the
$\sigma_1$-multiple degree-8 classes are $d_2$-boundaries, the rest die as
\emph{non-cycles}; the elaborating sentences are precise about exactly
this, so only the lead-in word is loose (``killed'' for ``boundaries'').

\textbf{F2 (nano, presentational).} Step~3's display presents the orbit
sums $S_1,S_2$ as named classes, but they are defined only up to
$\tau^3$-tails (the display chooses a splitting; only $A_1$ and the pinned
$\sigma_i$ are canonical). Step~7's quotient computation is nonetheless
\textbf{lift-independent}~--- the relation $2\tau^3=0$ kills the ambiguity
in the quotient, verified as a dedicated gate (M4)~--- and this is exactly
what makes the statement's ``discrimin\-ant class'' well defined in
$H^6(B;\Z)$ despite the R7-marked ambiguity in $H^6(\BK)$. The paper never
says so explicitly; half a clause in Step~7 would close it.

Neither finding is a defect in a claim; both are one-clause polish
opportunities of the same species as R7 was (a marking), strictly weaker:
nothing downstream depends on them.

\section*{5. Referee's summary}

The first pass asked whether the proof was \emph{correct}; this pass asked
whether the \emph{repaired} proof \emph{reads} as a proof --- whether the
eleven surgical hunks, each verified in isolation by their own waves,
compose into a text a reader can walk through without tripping over a
seam. They do. The forward-dependency structure is sound (and where it
exists it is either marked or, per F1, one clause short of being marked);
the load-bearing dependency created by the repairs (Step~5's completeness
resting on the R4 pin) is supplied rather than silent; the superseded
statements are gone everywhere; the corrected top-degree story is told
consistently in the statement, Steps 2/3/7, and the certificate remark;
and the two independent verification layers (W31's G3L, W32's twelve
gates) are now joined by this pass's 178 checks, which re-derive the whole
chain from the orbit decomposition up. The residue --- two optional
clauses --- is the honest size of what remains.

The mathematics, final state: $x^2\ne 0$ in $H^4(B;\Z)$; $x$ generating
$H^2$ and $x^2$ generating $H^4$; the additive tuple
$(\Z,0,\Z/3,\Z/3,\Z/3,\Z/3,\Z)$; and $H^6(B;\Z)\cong\Z$ with the
discriminant twice a generator --- hand-derived, machine-certified,
independently re-implemented, referee-verified, lattice-gated,
referee-complete, and now second-pass confirmed. \texttt{thm:state-d2} and
$\delta_2(\mathcal D(\C^3))=4/3$ untouched; the ququart bracket

\textbf{Overall recommendation: accept} --- the revision requested by
