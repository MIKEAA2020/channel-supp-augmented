# Journal-fit study (Wave 36 companion, 2026-09-14)

Live guideline + sample-article lookups backing the venue recommendation
delivered in conversation (all raw results in this folder):
- jmp.json — JMP editorial policy: "Manuscripts must be concise and
  comprehensible... minimal background" (length risk at 46/53 pp.).
- aop_guide.json — Annals of Physics guide for authors (fetched in full):
  "cover all fields of theoretical physics. Articles published in the
  journal are typically longer than 20 pages"; single-anonymized review.
- lmp.json + lmp_long.json — LMP: "letters and longer research articles";
  a 40-page accepted-paper example on arXiv.
- padova.json — Rendiconti Padova: "ready to accept long papers";
  mathematics scope (weak fit for the quantum framing).
- prxq_long.json + prxq_fee.json — PRX Quantum: "Research Articles (no
  length limit)"; gold OA, APC $3,590; highly selective.
- prr.json — APS length-limits page: PRR regular articles no length limit.
- quantum.json + quantum_fee.json + quantum_long.json + sample_kos.json —
  Quantum: community-run, highly selective, no page limit, publish free
  (optional reduced 100 EUR fee); sample: Kos-Styliaris, Quantum 7, 1020.

  (foundations of QM, quantum correlations, computation, mathematical
  physics section); content+length precedent: Regula, "Convex geometry of
  quantum resource quantification", J. Phys. A 51, 045303 (2018) — 45 pp.
- qip.json, cmp.json, cmp_sample.json, npjqi_len.json — the remaining
  venue scopes and the npj length-constraint evidence.
- content_borsuk.json, content_compress.json — where the neighbouring
  sub-field publishes (PRL theory notes, Quantum, IOP).
